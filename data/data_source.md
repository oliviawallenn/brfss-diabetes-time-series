# Data Source

## Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS)

Data used in this project were obtained from the
Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS),
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).

11 annual datasets were used (2013-2023) in the form of SAS
transport files (.xpt) downloaded per survey year

**Sample size:**~400,000+ respondents per year

Public-use BRFSS datasets:
https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/
